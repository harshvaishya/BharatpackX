## 🎯 Problem

Choosing suitable packaging is difficult because food commodities have different moisture, oxygen, respiration and storage requirements. Manual selection and limited packaging expertise can lead to material mismatch, spoilage, cost inefficiency and difficulty comparing sustainable alternatives.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

BharatPackX combines food-property analysis, a packaging knowledge database and an AI-assisted recommendation engine to suggest suitable materials.

- Collects commodity properties, storage conditions and transportation requirements.
- Supports image upload and voice-to-text descriptions.
- Matches food requirements with packaging-material properties.
- Compares OTR, WVTR, film thickness, gas permeability, MAP suitability, cost and sustainability.
- Provides material photos and listed supplier details.
- Uses local availability feedback to suggest alternatives when a material is unavailable.

### Core idea

> **From food commodity properties to a practical, cost-aware and sustainability-conscious packaging recommendation.**

## 🔄 Workflow

```text
Commodity Input (details / image / voice)
                 ↓
       Data Validation & Processing
                 ↓
       Food Property Analysis
                 ↓
     AI + Rule-Based Recommendation
                 ↓
     Packaging Knowledge Database
                 ↓
   Multi-Criteria Material Optimization
                 ↓
 Material + Cost + Shelf-Life + Sustainability
                 ↓
     Local Availability Feedback
          ↓                ↓
      Available      Suggest Alternatives
                 ↓
       Recommendation Dashboard
```

## 🧩 Key Components

- **Commodity analysis:** moisture, oil/fat content, pH, respiration, temperature, humidity and transport conditions.
- **Packaging database:** material specifications, barrier properties, OTR/WVTR, film thickness and mechanical properties.
- **Recommendation engine:** machine learning, pattern recognition, material matching and rule-based logic.
- **Optimization:** technical suitability, cost, sustainability and shelf-life requirements.
- **User output:** recommended material, specifications, material images, supplier details and alternatives.
